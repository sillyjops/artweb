About Me

As long as I can remember I've had a paintbrush in my hand. Drawing inspiration from anything and everything. I have always harboured an interest in transforming, reducing and investigating lines, shapes and colours of unassuming objects and everyday scenarios. Spotlighting the mundane to reveal hidden moments of connection and nostalgia.
I use everyday objects and food, people from my personal life and complete strangers as reference for my work. I aim to create interest and intrigue around subjects or moments of shared time that would otherwise be overlooked.
My work has been shown in galleries across the island of Ireland, such as Pallas Projects/Studios, Dublin (2024), The Braid Arts Centre, Ballymena (2025) and The Wexford Art Centre (2026).
